`scripts/seed_dev.py` people, password `dcp-dev`): the console in Chrome, the

(`settlementCode, structureId, hhId, headName, hhSize`), dataset key
`households`, key columns `settlementCode, structureId, hhId`. The page says

its sample row's values under the key, all `unassigned`.

**PM splits.** Three `S3` cases selected, holder Team A, Assign: "3 cases
assigned." `S4|1|1` to Team B: "1 case assigned." `S4|2|1` left in the pool.

**Supervisor A.** Signs in, opens the same page: "3 cases. You see the cases
held by your team." Exactly the three `S3` cases, Team A. No `S4|1|1`, no
`S4|2|1`. The upload form is not there. Assigns `S3|1|1` to Dev Enumerator:
the Person column fills in.

**Supervisor B.** "1 case." `S4|1|1`, Team B, nothing else. Assigns it to Dev
Enumerator B.

**The pool.** `S4|2|1` was on neither supervisor's page. The policy did that,
not the page — the page renders whatever `/cases` answers (break 188 is the

## The handset

**Sign in.** The install on the emulator was a v8 database from 7 September.
It opened on v9 with its four old submissions intact — migration 8 in place.
But it was stranded: registered locally against a server since recreated,
so sign-in said "sync once first" and sync got 401 before it could register.
Fixed on this branch (break 186); for the run, app data cleared, sync,
sign in as `enumerator`.

**Pull.** Sync: "Assigned to me (1)" — `S3|1|1`, `hhId: 1 · hhSize: 4 ·
headName: Amina Juma`, a Start chip. Not `S3|1|2` (a teammate's), not
`S3|2|1` (team-held, nobody's), not Team B's. The two submissions listed are
the enumerator's own from the morning's e2e — defect 26's rule holding.

**Collect.** Tap the case: the Household Survey opens with "Case S3|1|1"
under the title. Enumerator name typed, back, Sync: "All changes synced";
the case chip says "In progress"; the draft card says "Case S3|1|1". On the
server, one submission with `case_id` = the case, `created_by` = the
enumerator. Supervisor A's page now shows Collected 1 on `S3|1|1`.

**The case moves.** Supervisor A reassigns `S3|1|1` to themselves. On the
handset the draft is reopened and edited (one op pending), back, Sync.

**What the device did.** Sync succeeded. The section heading is now "No
longer assigned to you — drafts kept (1)" with `S3|1|1` under it, chip "No
longer assigned". The draft card reads, in red, "Case S3|1|1 · no longer
assigned to you" and "0 ops waiting to sync" — the pending op was pushed
and accepted (the server holds two ops on the submission). Tapping the
released case opens the draft. Consent answered Yes, back, Sync: accepted
again, three ops on the server. Finalising was refused by the form — six
required answers on the screens consent opens — which is the engine's rule
and not the case's; the finalize op would travel the path the third op just
did.

**`not_assigned`, end to end.** `S3|2|1` assigned to the enumerator, pulled
("Assigned to me (1)"), then moved to the supervisor while the handset was
not looking. Start tapped on the stale entry, an answer typed, Sync. The
sync bar, in red: "1 op refused: the case is no longer assigned to you. The
draft is kept on this device; it will sync once your supervisor assigns the
case back to you." The server answered 200 with `rejected: [{reason:
"not_assigned"}]`. The draft is listed under "No longer assigned to you —
drafts kept (2)" with "1 ops waiting to sync". Nothing lost, nothing
silent.
